黎明作戰 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日

「黎明作戰」（以下稱本遊戲）重視你的隱私。

我們自己不收集任何個人資料。本遊戲不需要帳號，也不會存取你的位置、聯絡人、相機、麥克風或手機上的其他檔案。遊戲進度與設定（例如音量、語言、已解鎖內容）只儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳送給我們。解除安裝本遊戲或清除 App 資料時，這些資料會一併刪除。

廣告（Google AdMob）

Android 版有可自由選擇觀看的獎勵廣告：機體被擊毀時可以看廣告復活，標題畫面也可以看廣告領取金幣。看不看完全由你決定，不看也能正常遊戲。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供。為了顯示與衡量廣告、防止詐騙，Google 可能會收集並處理你的裝置資訊，例如廣告 ID、IP 位址、裝置型號與作業系統、廣告互動紀錄等。這些資料由 Google 依照其政策處理，我們拿不到你的個人資料：

  • Google 隱私權政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

在歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士等需要取得同意的地區，第一次開啟遊戲時會顯示 Google 的同意畫面，你可以選擇是否允許個人化廣告。你也可以隨時在手機的「設定 → Google → 廣告」中重設或刪除廣告 ID。

兒童

本遊戲不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的任何資料。

OPERATION REIMEI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

We do not collect any personal data ourselves. The game needs no account and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or other files. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android version offers optional rewarded ads: you can watch one to revive when your fighter is destroyed, or to get coins from the title screen. Watching is always your choice, and the game continues normally if you don't. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and OS, and ad interactions, under Google's own policies. We never receive your personal data.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where consent is required (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown on first launch so you can choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
